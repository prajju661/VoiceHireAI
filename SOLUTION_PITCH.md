# VoiceHire AI — Your Personal Adaptive AI Interviewer

### Project Overview
VoiceHire AI is an AI-powered interview practice platform that helps candidates prepare for technical interviews through interactive, voice-based conversations. It provides adaptive questions, answer evaluation, personalized feedback and performance reports.

### Problem Statement
Traditional interview preparation often relies on static question banks and text-based practice. Candidates may lack realistic speaking practice, immediate feedback and guidance on areas where they need improvement.

### Proposed Solution
VoiceHire AI provides an interactive interview experience where candidates select a technical role, difficulty level and preferred language. The platform asks questions through voice, accepts spoken answers, converts speech into text and evaluates responses to provide useful feedback and adaptive follow-up questions.

### Key Features
- Technical interview practice with role and difficulty selection.
- English, Telugu and Telugu-English language modes.
- Voice-based interview questions and spoken answers.
- Speech transcription and answer evaluation.
- Adaptive follow-up questions.
- Feedback highlighting strengths and improvement areas.
- Final interview performance reports and saved interview history.

### Gnani AI Integrations
- **Gnani Prisma STT:** Converts candidates' spoken responses into text.
- **Gnani Timbre TTS:** Generates spoken interview questions.

The current evaluation and question-adaptation logic uses a rule-based reasoning engine. Gnani Evon integration is not included because access is still pending.

### Technology Stack
React, TypeScript, Vite, Python, FastAPI and SQLite, integrated with Gnani AI APIs.

### Expected Impact
VoiceHire AI aims to make technical interview preparation more interactive and accessible by combining voice-based practice, structured feedback and adaptive questioning in one platform.
